# Parasite Dataset

## Description
 A dataset consisting of 34,298 microscopic images of multiple parasites (Plasmodium, Toxoplasma, Babesia, Leishmania, Trypanosome, Trichomonad) and host cells (Red blood cell, and Leukocyte) under 400X or 1000X magnification. the original dataset includes 843 images of Plasmodium and 3,758  images of T.gondii at 400X, and 2,933 images of Toxoplasma, 1,173 images of Babesia, 2,701 images of Leishmania, 2,385 images of Trypanosome, and 10,134 images of Trichomonad at 1000X. Besides, this dataset also contains 8,995 images of Red blood cells and 461 images of Leukocyte at 1000X, and  915 images of Leukocyte captured at 400X.  
### Source:
[Microscopic Images of Parasites Species](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/38jtn4nzs6/3)
### <span style="color: red;">Important note:</span>
I consolidated the four sets of directories—Leukocyte at 1000X, Leukocyte at 400X, Toxoplasma at 1000X, and Toxoplasma at 400X—into two organized folders: Leukocyte and Toxoplasma. Each folder now contains all the images across both 1000X and 400X magnifications.
